    Install CiviScribe.

    Find it in ComfyUI Manager, install the node pack, then restart ComfyUI.

    Connect your finished IMAGE.

    Add “CiviScribe – Save Image for Civitai” and connect your workflow’s final image.

  3. ✓

    Run. Save. Share when you’re ready.

    The PNG defaults are a great place to start. CiviScribe saves locally; you decide what to upload.

THREE FORMATS. ONE SOURCE OF TRUTH.

Pick the right
travel companion.

All three formats carry generation metadata. Your priorities decide the container.

PNGThe everyday favorite

The whole story.

Lossless pixels, transparency, rich metadata, and optional reloadable ComfyUI workflow payloads.

Explore PNG
JPEGThe familiar sharer

Quality takes the lead.

Quality 100 and 4:4:4 chroma defaults. Lossy encoding; transparent pixels flatten onto your chosen background.

Explore JPEG
WebPThe light packer

Lossless by default.

Preserves decoded 8-bit samples and transparency in lossless mode, with generation metadata in EXIF.
